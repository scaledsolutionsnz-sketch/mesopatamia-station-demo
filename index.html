Our Story

A Living Piece of
New Zealand History

Founded in 1860 by the celebrated English writer Samuel Butler — author of Erewhon — Mesopotamia Station sits at the headwaters of the Rangitata Gorge, nestled between two rivers amid the breathtaking Southern Alps. Butler named it Mesopotamia, meaning "land between two rivers," a name it has carried with pride for over 165 years.

The Prouting family have stewarded Mesopotamia since 1945, welcoming generations of guests to share in what makes this place so rare — the sheer scale of the wilderness, the quality of the hunting, and the warmth of genuine high-country hospitality.
